Direct Family Connection

You'll Talk to Us—Not a Call Center.

When you search for senior care, big chains pass you through admissions advisors and call scripts. At Cedar Creek Care Home, the person who answers your call is the person overseeing your parent's meals, safety, and comfort every day.

With a strict maximum of 6 residents and a 1:3 caregiver ratio, there is no rushing down long hallways. Caregivers know when your dad likes his coffee, which songs calm your mom, and text you photos of sunny afternoons on the porch.

Urgent Discharge Placement

Coming From the Hospital or Rehab?

Discharge planners often give families only 24 to 48 hours to find safe care. We simplify every step so your loved one transitions smoothly.

01

Same-Day Care Review

Call us directly. We speak with you and your hospital case manager the same day to review mobility, medications, and room readiness.

02

Suite & Med Preparation

We ready a furnished private suite, coordinate discharge prescriptions with our local pharmacy, and set up walkers or hospital beds if needed.

03

Peaceful Move-In

Your loved one arrives within 24–48 hours to a warm meal, calm quiet rooms, and attentive caregivers ready to help them rest.
